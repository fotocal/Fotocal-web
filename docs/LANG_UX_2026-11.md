## 1. The language switcher works again (it was dead everywhere)

- **The cause:** the header is now baked into every page, but the script only wired up the menu when it built the menu itself. On top of that, a "done" marker had been baked into the saved header too, so even a fixed script skipped it.
- **The fix:** the menu opens and closes, closes with Escape or a click outside, and remembers the choice (`fotocal-lang`).
- **Where it goes:** every language link leads to the SAME page in the other language, never the home page.
- **On phones:** the ☰ menu now has **Español / English** buttons as well.
- **Tested** on desktop in both languages, and from the phone menu, on: the home page, a feature page, the blog and a post, the calculator hub and a calculator, the dish hub, a dish page, the full table, privacy, subscription and FAQ.
- **New check:** `tools/check_lang.mjs` stops this from breaking again.

## 2. Every page now has an English twin

- **New English pages:** the dish hub, all 53 dish pages and the full table of 218 foods.
  - They show the same numbers, because both languages come from the same function.
  - Dish names stay recognisable, e.g. "Paella valenciana (Valencian paella)", and the table shows "Spanish name (English name)".
  - The English dish pages are built from the data: the serving, where the energy comes from, what is in it, how it compares, and 3 questions answered from the figures. The long Spanish prose on each dish was not translated.
- **The five Spanish-only blog posts** were translated: tapas, Spanish breakfast, menú del día, losing a kilo, and the 500 kcal deficit.
- **Head tags:** hreflang es/en/x-default and canonical are correct on both sides, checked by the site check.
- **Sitemap:** 286 URLs, up from 226: 60 new English URLs, listed at the end.

## 3. Visitors land in their own language

I chose the **automatic redirect on the first visit**, not the banner. It does what you asked, and the safeguards remove the SEO risk:
- Search engines and other bots never get redirected, so Google keeps seeing both versions, linked by hreflang. Lighthouse and headless browsers are skipped too.
- Someone arriving from a search engine is never redirected, because Google already chose the right language for them.
- It happens only once per browser. After that, the language they read or picked is respected, and `?lang=es` / `?lang=en` overrides everything.
- It runs as the first thing on the page, so there is no flash of the wrong language.

Tested:
- English browser on a Spanish page → English twin
- Spanish browser on an English page → Spanish
- French browser → stays put
- Googlebot → stays put
- visitor from Google → stays put
- `?lang=es` → stays Spanish

## 4. Calculators are obviously tools now

### What changed

- **Hub:** it opens with "Calcula en segundos, gratis y sin registro" and a quick BMI tool (height + weight → result). Then come the 8 cards, each with an icon, one plain line and a **"Usar calculadora"** button.
- **Each calculator:**
  - The tool card sits right under the title.
  - On a 390 px phone the whole form and the **Calcular** button are on the first screen. Pressing it shows the result and scrolls it into view. After that the result follows every change.
  - **Inputs start empty**, with examples ("ej. 70"). The visitor's own earlier entries or a shared link still fill them in.
  - **A wrong or empty field** shows a short message under it (e.g. "Introduce un peso entre 35 y 250 kg."), and the old result is dimmed so it never looks current.
  - **Activity options** use plain words ("Sedentario: trabajo sentado, poco movimiento"). The ×1,2–×1,725 factors moved to a small "¿Cómo se calcula?" note.
  - **Fields appear only when they matter:** macros shows "¿A qué ritmo?" only for perder/ganar peso, and BMR shows the body-fat row only when body fat is filled in.
  - **Readability:** result text is at least 13 px, and the main number is big, in the accent colour.
  - **Layout:** on desktop the form and the result sit side by side, with the explanation below, full width. The FAQ starts collapsed.
- **Download card under every result:**
  - It has the app icon, a headline with the result ("¿Quieres cumplir tus 1.918 kcal sin pesar la comida?") and the line "Haz una foto y Fotocal cuenta las calorías por ti."
  - The button is big, at least 52 px tall, with the Google Play mark: "Descárgala gratis".
  - Under it: "Gratis · sin tarjeta · Android", plus the free-plan limits.
  - It slides in gently when the first result appears (no animation for people who turn motion off).
  - The same card is on every dish page, the dish hub and the calculator hub.
  - The links keep their UTM tags.
- **Phone download bar:** it now has the app icon, and appears only once there is a result and it has scrolled away.

## 5. Number fixes

- **Dish servings:** both columns now come from the one weight the calories belong to. Paella is 307 g = 515 kcal = 168 per 100 g.
  - A script checked all 53 dish tables and all 218 table rows: 0 mismatches.
  - It also caught 9 dishes whose text stated the old weight; that text now follows the real weight.
- **Rounding:** the calorie target is now rounded like maintenance, so maintenance minus target always equals the deficit shown.
  - Compared with the app's own code on 20,000 random profiles: calories within 1 kcal (the app floors), fat within 1 g and carbs within 2 g (knock-on of that 1 kcal), and protein exactly equal.
- **Healthy weight:** "above/below the range" is measured from the exact limit and shown in whole kilos ("unos 8 kg"; "menos de 1 kg" when that's the case).
- **Stale text:** the dish hub now says "las ocho calculadoras".
- **Dish order:** the dish hub is grouped into Platos y guisos, then Tapas, raciones y bocadillos, then Desayunos, dulces y postres, then Pan, aceite, fruta y frutos secos, with **Bebidas last**. Coca-Cola Zero is no longer first.
