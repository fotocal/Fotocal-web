# Fotocal: directory and profile listings

Date checked: 2026-11-08. All sites below were found through web search on that date. Page fetching was blocked from the research sandbox, so authority scores and link attributes (dofollow / nofollow) could not be read from the pages. They are marked "n/a" and "unknown". After you publish each listing, check the link yourself: right-click > Inspect and look for `rel="nofollow"`, `rel="ugc"` or `rel="sponsored"`.

Rules for every listing:

- Use the same name everywhere: **Fotocal**. Use the same URL: **https://getfotocal.com/**. Use the same category: Health & Fitness / Nutrition / Calorie counter.
- Only state the facts in the "Copy-paste descriptions" section. Do not add ratings, download counts, testimonials or awards.
- Record each submission in `tracker.csv` the day you send it (status: to do → submitted → live / rejected).

## Copy-paste descriptions

Use these exactly. Do not add numbers you cannot prove.

### Spanish, short (≤160 characters)

> Fotocal cuenta las calorías de tu comida con una foto. App Android con escáner de códigos de barras, registro por voz y coach IA. Plan gratis sin tarjeta.

### Spanish, long (~120 words)

> Fotocal es una app para Android que cuenta las calorías de tu comida a partir de una foto del plato. Además puedes escanear códigos de barras, registrar comidas por voz y consultar a Coach Kal, un coach con inteligencia artificial. El plan gratuito no pide tarjeta ni caduca: incluye 2 escaneos de fotos con IA al día, 1 registro por voz al día, y escáner de códigos de barras y Coach Kal ilimitados. Premium cuesta 6,99 €/mes o 34,99 €/año, con 5 días de prueba. En la web hay 8 calculadoras gratuitas (calorías diarias, déficit calórico, IMC, proteínas, macros, agua, TMB y peso saludable) y una tabla gratuita de calorías de platos españoles. Fotocal lo hace una sola persona en Madrid.
>
> Web: https://getfotocal.com/ · Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fotokal.app

### English, short (≤160 characters)

> Fotocal counts the calories in your food from a photo. Android app with barcode scanner, voice logging and an AI coach. Free plan, no card needed.

### English, long (~120 words)

> Fotocal is an Android app that counts the calories in your food from a photo of your plate. You can also scan barcodes, log meals by voice and ask Coach Kal, an AI coach. The free plan needs no card and never expires: it includes 2 AI photo scans a day, 1 voice log a day, and unlimited barcode scanning and Coach Kal. Premium costs €6.99/month or €34.99/year, with a 5-day trial. The website also offers 8 free calculators (daily calories, calorie deficit, BMI, protein, macros, water, BMR and healthy weight) and a free table of calories in Spanish dishes, with 53 dish pages and a 218-food table. Fotocal is made by one person in Madrid.
>
> Web: https://getfotocal.com/ · Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fotokal.app
