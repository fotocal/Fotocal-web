## Why this matters (plain English)

Links from other websites ("backlinks"), together with time, are among the biggest ranking factors for a new site. Google treats a link from a relevant, trusted site as a vote of confidence. A young domain like getfotocal.com has very few votes, so even excellent pages rank low at first. Mentions of the brand name without a link also help Google understand that "Fotocal" is a real entity.

**Expect results over months, not days.** A link usually has to be found, crawled and indexed before it counts, and ranking gains tend to show up 2–6 months later. Steady work (a few honest links and mentions each month) beats a burst.

**What to avoid:** do not buy links, do not join link exchanges or "link schemes" (I link you, you link me, at scale), do not use private blog networks (PBNs), and do not pay for "guest posts" on sites that sell posts to anyone. Google's spam policies explicitly target these. The usual result is that the links are ignored, and in bad cases the site gets a manual penalty that is hard to recover from. If a widget or sponsorship ever involves payment, the link must be marked `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`. Free tools (calculators, the Spanish-dish table) that people link to because they are useful are the safe path to rankings that last.
