### How to answer journalist requests honestly

1. **Answer only what you actually know.** You are an app maker, not a dietitian or doctor. Do not give medical or clinical advice. Talk about building a photo-based calorie app, what AI photo estimation can and cannot do, Spanish dishes, and being a solo founder.
2. **Say who you are.** "Solo developer of Fotocal, an Android calorie-counting app (Madrid)." Nothing inflated: no "CEO of a leading…", no user numbers.
3. **Be upfront about the limits of AI.** Photo-based estimates can be wrong, especially for hidden fats and portion size (recent Spanish coverage, e.g. Vozpópuli and wwwhatsnew, reports that AI calorie apps underestimate). Saying this is honest and gets you quoted more often.
4. **Keep it short:** 2–4 sentences the journalist can quote, plus your name, title, URL and one link.
5. **Never use AI-written answers you have not checked, fake credentials or invented data.**
6. **Answer within hours, not days.** Late answers rarely get used.

**Example answer (English):**

> Name: [you] - Solo developer of Fotocal, an Android calorie-counting app, Madrid (https://getfotocal.com/)
>
> "Photo-based calorie counting is good at recognising what's on the plate, but it struggles with things a camera can't see: the oil in a sofrito, the size of the portion, a sauce underneath. That's why I think it works best as a quick first estimate that the user can correct, not as an exact measurement. For Spanish home cooking, oil is the big blind spot."

## (d) Reddit, Quora ES and Spanish forums

Places: r/spain (https://www.reddit.com/r/spain/), r/askspain (https://www.reddit.com/r/askspain/), Quora en español (https://es.quora.com/), Forocoches (https://forocoches.com/), plus fitness subforums where self-promotion is allowed. **Read each community's rules before posting.** Many ban self-promotion entirely or only allow it in specific threads.

### Rules for honest answers

1. **Help first.** Answer the actual question fully without needing a link. If the answer only works with your link, do not post.
2. **Always disclose:** "Disclaimer: soy el desarrollador de Fotocal." Put it in the same comment, never hidden.
3. **Link only when it is truly helpful,** and prefer the free web resource (calculator, Spanish-dish table) over the app download.
4. **Mention alternatives** when relevant (e.g. that FatSecret has a strong free plan, or that Cronometer tracks many nutrients). Recommending only yourself looks like spam.
5. **One account, your real identity.** No sock puppets, no upvote requests, no "friends" posting for you, no buying aged accounts.
6. **At most 1–2 helpful answers a week.** Volume posting gets accounts banned and the domain flagged.
7. **Reddit and Forocoches links are nofollow.** The value is real visitors and brand mentions, not PageRank.
