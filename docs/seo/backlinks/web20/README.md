## What Web 2.0 links are

"Web 2.0" means free platforms where anyone can publish: Blogger, WordPress.com, Medium, Tumblr, LinkedIn, Dev.to and so on. You write a post there, and the post links to your site.

## What they are NOT

- **They are not a ranking shortcut.** Links from these platforms usually carry very little weight. Many of them (Medium, LinkedIn, Tumblr, Dev.to in many cases) add `nofollow` or similar tags, so Google may pass little or no ranking value through them.
- **They do not replace real links.** The links that move rankings come from real sites that choose to mention you: press, nutrition blogs, app reviews, partners.

## What they do help with (a little)

- **Discovery.** Google and people can find getfotocal.com from another place.
- **Brand mentions.** "Fotocal" appears on trusted platforms, next to useful content in Spanish.
- **Content you can reuse.** Each article is a ready script for an Instagram carousel, a TikTok or a YouTube Short.
- **Real readers.** A good article on Medium, LinkedIn or Dev.to can bring a few people who actually install the app.

## Rules — please follow these

1. **One post per platform. Never more.** Do not create dozens of thin posts or blogs. Google treats networks of low-quality posts made only to link to a site as a *link scheme*. At best it ignores them. At worst it can act against your site.
2. **Space them out.** One article every two weeks (schedule below). Not all six on the same day.
3. **Do not repost the same article** on two platforms. Each text is written for one place only. (Dev.to *or* Hashnode, never both.)
4. **Do not copy text from getfotocal.com** into these posts, and do not paste these posts onto getfotocal.com. Duplicate text helps nobody.
5. **Keep the facts exact.** Only what is true: Android app, photo calorie counting, barcode scanner, voice logging, Coach Kal, made by one person in Madrid, free plan (no card, no expiry: 2 AI photo scans a day, 1 voice log a day, barcode scanner and Coach Kal unlimited), Premium €6.99/month or €34.99/year with a 5-day trial, Google Play only. Never add ratings, download numbers, testimonials or awards.
6. **Max two links to getfotocal.com per article.** They are already in place. Do not add more.
7. **Use your real name and your own photos.** No stock photos copied from Google Images.
8. **Reply to comments** on each platform during the first week.

## How these posts get found (Search Console will not do it)

Search Console's **URL Inspection → Request indexing** only works for URLs on your own verified site (getfotocal.com). You **cannot** use it for a Blogger, Medium or LinkedIn URL.

So the way to get each post discovered is to **share it**:

- Post it on **Instagram** (carousel or Reel; put the post URL in your bio link or a Story link sticker for a week).
- Post a short video on **TikTok** (link in profile).
- Upload the same video as a **YouTube Short** with the URL in the description.
- Share the link on your **Facebook** page.
- **Link to it from the next article** in the schedule. Each posting guide tells you the exact Spanish line to add at the end of the new article. This chains the posts together, so finding one leads to the others.

Each platform file has the exact share ideas for that article.

## After all six are out

- Do **not** start a second round of Web 2.0 posts. Six good posts is enough.
- Spend the time on things that matter more: getting real sites to mention Fotocal, improving getfotocal.com pages, and making more short videos.
- Check Search Console → **Links** once a month. Some of these links may appear there after a few weeks; nofollow ones may never appear. Both are normal.
