**Shared patterns of the top pages, to copy honestly:**
1. **Dish pages:** title = "[Plato] — Calorías por 100 g / ración" with the kcal number in the meta description. The H1 often includes the kcal.
2. **Comparison pages:** a year in the title ("en 2026"), the number of apps, prices for Spain, "cómo elegimos", "cuándo es mejor la otra app", and a changelog.
3. **Calculators:** the formula name (Harris-Benedict / Mifflin-St Jeor), sexo (mujer/hombre), "gratis / sin registro", a worked example, and a table.
4. **Medical-adjacent queries:** a bibliography or sources section, and an author or reviewer.
5. **Feature pages:** "cómo funciona", "¿qué precisión tiene?", "gratis / sin registro", plus an FAQ.
