Made for your goal

One app, your reasons.

Whatever brought you here — losing weight, eating smarter, or finally making habits stick — the same nine features bend to your goal.

For weight loss

No crash diets, no banned foods — a repeatable loop that fits real life. Fotocal sets a realistic target aimed at a safe ~0.5–1 kg per week, the photo scan makes logging effortless enough to survive busy days, your calorie ring keeps you in a gentle deficit, and the trend chart smooths out the scale's daily noise so you see the real direction.

  • Realistic calorie & macro targets from your goal
  • Calorie ring shows the room left in your day
  • Trend chart, not scale panic
  • Coach Kal for plateaus and eating out

For nutrition & diet

Understand what you eat, not just how much. Every meal becomes a full nutrition picture — macros, micronutrients, health score and healthier swaps — and Fotocal never forces one diet on you: it tracks the numbers so your chosen approach becomes easy to follow.

  • Works with high-protein, low-carb, vegetarian or balanced eating
  • Health score teaches what "good" looks like

For lifestyle & mindset

Small wins, repeated — that's the whole method. A photo makes logging effortless, streaks turn consistency into a game worth protecting, a quick mood note reveals how food and feelings connect, and the weekly report celebrates what went well instead of punishing what didn't. One indulgent meal doesn't undo progress; quitting does.
