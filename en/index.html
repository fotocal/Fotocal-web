How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

Logging a meal takes about as long as taking a photo — because that is all it is. These are real screens from the app.

  1. Snap a photo

    Open Fotocal and photograph your plate. Home-cooked, restaurant, or last night's leftovers — it all works.

  2. The AI reads it

    In seconds it identifies every ingredient, estimates the portion, and works out calories, macros and a health score.

  3. It's logged

    The meal drops straight into your diary and your whole day updates itself — calories left, macros, water, streak. Done.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate is the photo scanning?

Fotocal uses current AI vision models to identify food and estimate portions, and it is good enough that the numbers are genuinely useful day to day. It is an estimate, not a laboratory measurement — mixed dishes, stews and anything layered or hidden under sauce are the hardest cases. You can always correct the portion or the ingredients before you save, and a corrected estimate is still far faster than searching a database.

Do I have to weigh my food?

No. Estimating the portion from the photo is the whole point. If you want to be precise on a particular meal you can enter the weight by hand, but nothing requires it.

What else can I scan besides my plate?

Plenty. Scan a product's barcode to get its full nutrition and a clear health score before you buy it, point the camera at a restaurant menu to see estimated calories per dish, or simply say what you ate out loud and Fotocal logs it. Photo, barcode, menu or voice — whichever is fastest right then.

Is Fotocal free?

Downloading Fotocal is free, and you can stay on the free plan for good. Premium starts with a 5-day free trial and removes the daily scan and voice counters, adds the menu scanner, the full nutrient panel, the rest of the weekly report and a daily plan. Coach Kal is free on every plan. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. After that, Premium is €6.99 a month or €34.99 a year.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play, so you cancel there: Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel before the 5-day trial ends and you are not charged.

What happens to my data?

Your data is yours. We never sell it, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Which languages does the app support?

English and Spanish, in the app and on this site. Fotocal is built Spanish-first, so the Spanish experience is a first-class citizen rather than an afterthought translation.

Does Fotocal track my steps?

Yes. Fotocal connects to Health Connect on Android, so your steps sync automatically and feed into your daily energy picture alongside your TDEE.

Which is the best calorie counter?

The one you keep using. Three things decide it: it should know the food you actually eat, Spanish dishes included; fixing a number should take seconds; and the free plan should be useful without a card. Fotocal's free plan gives 2 AI scans and 1 voice log a day, with the barcode scanner, Coach Kal and the weekly report unlimited. If that covers your day, it is the best one for you; if you scan more, Premium removes the two limits.

Which calorie counter is free?

Fotocal is, with no expiry and no card: 2 AI photo scans and 1 voice log a day, plus the barcode scanner, Coach Kal, the diary, weight, water, steps and the weekly report without limits. Premium is optional, €6.99 a month or €34.99 a year with a 5-day trial on both plans, and it only removes the two daily limits and opens the deeper nutrient panels.
