Worth saying plainly

Fotocal counts and explains. It does not prescribe.

There is a lot of advice about what people should eat. This is not any of it.

  • No way of eating is recommended over another anywhere in the app or on this site. The list above is a list, not a ranking.
  • The score rates the product in front of the camera. It is not a mark for the day, the week or the person holding the phone.
  • Nothing is forbidden and nothing is labelled a treat or a cheat. A logged meal is a logged meal.
  • The figures are estimates. A portion read from a photo, a database value and the app's own arithmetic each carry error.

Fotocal is not a medical device, and this is not dietary advice

If you are pregnant, living with diabetes or another condition, taking medication, under eighteen, or have any history of an eating disorder, speak to a doctor or a registered dietitian before changing how you eat or using a calorie tracker. Their advice comes first, and a tracking app is the wrong tool for some people.

Questions about the nutrition side

What does the score out of 100 actually measure?

It rates the food itself, and the app lists its reasons under the number so you can see what drove it rather than taking it on trust. It says nothing about you, your day or your week, and a low score is not an instruction to put something back.

How many vitamins and minerals do I see on the free plan?

Two. The scan reads the whole list either way, and the free plan shows the first two with the rest dimmed under an unlock. The fibre, sugar, sodium and saturated fat figures and the processing level are in the same locked part of the screen.

Does Fotocal tell me what to eat?

No. It shows you what a meal contains and, after a scan, offers one alternative you could have instead. Coach Kal answers questions you ask it. None of that is a plan, and no way of eating is pushed over another.

Do I have to pick a diet?

No. There is an option for no diet at all, and leaving the health profile blank is fine — the app works without it. If you do set one, four of the options change the macro split the targets are built from, and all of them shape what Coach Kal suggests.
